Color Vision Deficiency (CVD) Proofing & Simulation Simulated Spectrum

Approximately 8% of male warehouse workers and logistics drivers live with congenital color blindness. The system guarantees complete operational usability across the four clinical types:

Protanopia (Red-Blind) Proofed

Absence of long-wavelength red retinal photoreceptors. Critical hazard: Red warnings appear as dark brown/black.

Nexa Rule: Red warnings always carry a distinctive octagon shape and bold warning icon (\(\Delta L > 30\%\)).
Deuteranopia (Green-Blind) Proofed

Absence of medium-wavelength green cones (most common, ~6%). Hazard: Confusion between success green and pending amber.

Nexa Rule: Green uses check-circle glyph; Amber uses clock glyph. Surface lightness differs by at least 15%.
Tritanopia (Blue-Yellow) Proofed

Rare absence of short-wavelength cones. Blue is perceived as teal/grey, while yellow appears light grey/pink.

Nexa Rule: Blue primary actions maintain heavy stroke boundaries and distinct text labels.
Achromatopsia (Monochromacy) Proofed

Total absence of color cone function. The entire interface is perceived purely as grayscale luminance values.

Nexa Rule: 100% of data density and state hierarchy is conveyed through spatial position, typography and icons.

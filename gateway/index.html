02 / PRINCIPLES · ARCHITECTURAL LAWS & QUAD-CUE

Core Principles & The Quad-Cue Feedback Law

Operational design laws governing industrial logistics software. In warehouse and field operations, zero decorative luxury is permitted in task flows: every element must prioritize cognitive throughput, error mitigation, and uncompromising accessibility under high-glare ambient lighting.

1. Operational Velocity Speed First

Sub-100ms visual perception. Primary operational flows (picking, barcode wedge scan, route dispatch) require zero blocking dialogs, minimal animation delay, and deterministic keyboard/hardware wedge shortcuts.

2. Cognitive Clarity Density · Zero Noise

High data-to-ink ratio. Single-divider law prevents double-bordered visual clutter. Pure opaque layered surfaces replace low-contrast transparent blurs or gradients in operational task windows.

3. Resilient Autonomy Offline-First

Designed for field failure modes. Optimistic local state persistence, background sync queues, and hardware barcode wedge listeners ensure warehouse terminals never freeze when cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity drops.

4. Uncompromising Accessibility WCAG 2.2 AAA

Strict target size minimums (44px/48dp, 56dp gloved), APCA mathematical contrast certification (>17:1 on core text), and the inviolable Quad-Cue rule guaranteeing zero reliance on color alone.

The Quad-Cue Feedback Law (WCAG 1.4.1 Compliant) 4 Cues Enforced

In variable warehouse lighting (10 to 100,000 lux) and dirty visor conditions, color alone fails. Every critical status indicator must simultaneously provide four redundant sensory cues: (1) Surface Tint + (2) Distinct 1px Border + (3) High-Contrast Label + (4) Pinned Line Icon.

Color Vision Deficiency (CVD) Proofing & Simulation Simulated Spectrum

Approximately 8% of male warehouse workers and logistics drivers live with congenital color blindness. The system guarantees complete operational usability across the four clinical types:

Protanopia (Red-Blind) Proofed

Absence of long-wavelength red retinal photoreceptors. Critical hazard: Red warnings appear as dark brown/black.

Nexa Rule: Red warnings always carry a distinctive octagon shape and bold warning icon (\(\Delta L > 30\%\)).
Deuteranopia (Green-Blind) Proofed

Absence of medium-wavelength green cones (most common, ~6%). Hazard: Confusion between success green and pending amber.

Nexa Rule: Green uses check-circle glyph; Amber uses clock glyph. Surface lightness differs by at least 15%.
Tritanopia (Blue-Yellow) Proofed

Rare absence of short-wavelength cones. Blue is perceived as teal/grey, while yellow appears light grey/pink.

Nexa Rule: Blue primary actions maintain heavy stroke boundaries and distinct text labels.
Achromatopsia (Monochromacy) Proofed

Total absence of color cone function. The entire interface is perceived purely as grayscale luminance values.

Nexa Rule: 100% of data density and state hierarchy is conveyed through spatial position, typography and icons.

import 'package:flutter/material.dart';
import 'package:nexa_ui/tokens/nexa_tokens.dart';

class NexaButton extends StatelessWidget {
  final String label;
  final VoidCallback? onPressed;
  final IconData? leadingIcon;
  final bool isFullWidth;

  const NexaButton({
    super.key,
    required this.label,
    required this.onPressed,
    this.leadingIcon,
    this.isFullWidth = true,
  });

  @override
  Widget build(BuildContext context) {
    final tokens = Theme.of(context).extension<NexaThemeTokens>()!;
    return SizedBox(
      height: NexaSizes.controlHeightLg,
      width: isFullWidth ? double.infinity : null,
      child: FilledButton.icon(
        onPressed: onPressed,
        style: FilledButton.styleFrom(
          backgroundColor: tokens.primary600,
          foregroundColor: tokens.textInverse,
          shape: RoundedRectangleBorder(

          ),

        icon: leadingIcon != null ? Icon(leadingIcon, size: 18) : const SizedBox.shrink(),
